Frequently Asked Questions About the Auto Train

Everything you need to know before packing your car and boarding the train.

Can I leave luggage packed inside my vehicle?

Yes! One of the biggest perks of the Auto Train is that you can pack your vehicle with as much luggage, golf equipment, strollers, or household items as you wish with zero weight restrictions. Just pack a small overnight carry-on for the passenger car.

How does vehicle loading and offloading work?

When you arrive at the terminal, certified auto rail personnel carefully drive your car onto specialized enclosed bi-level auto racks. Your car is securely locked inside during transit and driven off by crew at destination.

What is the Priority Offloading option?

Priority Offloading guarantees that your vehicle will be among the first 30 vehicles unloaded from the auto carriers upon arrival, minimizing your wait time so you can hit the road right away. Priority spaces are limited—call our desk to add it.

Are meals included on the Auto Train?

Passengers in private sleeper cabins (Roomettes, Bedrooms, Family Bedrooms) receive complimentary chef-prepared three-course hot dinners and continental breakfasts. Coach passengers can purchase hot meals, snacks, and beverages in the Cafe Lounge.

Can I bring my pet on the Auto Train?

Small cats and dogs up to 20 lbs (including pet carrier) are welcome for an additional pet fee on journeys up to seven hours in designated coaches or private roomettes. Speak with our agent for current pet reservation guidelines.

Can passengers travel without a vehicle?

No. By federal railway regulation, all passengers on the Auto Train must have an accompanying motor vehicle booked on the same train. If traveling without a car, our specialists can book you on the Silver Meteor or Silver Star instead.
