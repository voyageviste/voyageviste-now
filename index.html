CANCELLATION & REFUND GUIDELINES

REFUND POLICY

Fair and transparent cancellation and refund policy across flights, hotels, trains, and cruises.

REFUND POLICY

At VoyageViste.com, we understand that plans can change. That’s why we aim to provide a fair and transparent cancellation and refund policy. Please read this carefully before making your booking.

Booking Cancellations

1. Airline Tickets

  • Cancellation Rules Vary by Airline: Each airline has its own cancellation policies, fare rules, and refund timelines.
  • Refundable vs. Non-Refundable Tickets: Refundable Tickets may be eligible for a full or partial refund. Non-Refundable Tickets usually cannot be refunded, but in some cases may be eligible for credit towards a future trip (airline rules apply).
  • 24-Hour Free Cancellation (when applicable): If your booking qualifies, you may cancel within 24 hours of purchase without penalty.

2. Hotel & Train Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations are subject to the provider's individual policies. Some may allow free cancellations within a specific time frame, while others may charge a fee or be non-refundable.
  • Train tickets are subject to the cancellation policy of the railway carrier (e.g., VIA Rail, Amtrak, etc.).
  • Cancellation eligibility depends on the fare class, route, and carrier rules.
  • Flexible or refundable fares may allow cancellation for a partial or full refund.
  • Non-refundable fares cannot be canceled for a refund but may be eligible for credit or date change (if allowed by the provider).

Cruise Cancellation Policy

Cruise bookings are governed by the cruise line’s cancellation terms (e.g., Royal Caribbean, Carnival, MSC, etc.). Cancellation penalties vary based on how far in advance the request is made before the departure date.

General guideline (may vary by cruise line):

  • 91+ days before departure: Full refund (minus service fees)
  • 61–90 days: Partial refund or credit
  • 31–60 days: Heavier penalties may apply
  • 30 days or less: Usually non-refundable

Refund Policy

  • Processing Time: Refunds (if applicable) are typically processed within 7–21 business days. However, it can vary based on the airline, train, or cruise providers.
  • Refund Method: Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used during booking.
  • Service Fees: VoyageViste service fees are non-refundable, unless the error was caused on our end.

Important Notes

  • All cancellation or refund requests must be submitted via email or phone.
  • Changes or cancellations are only processed during our regular support hours.
  • Some promotional or discounted fares are strictly non-changeable and non-refundable.
